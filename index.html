Income statement

Includes both regular and adjusting entries for the period you choose.

Statement of owner's equity

Equity accounts with "drawing" in the name are treated as owner withdrawals; all other equity accounts count as capital.

Statement of financial position

Asset and liability balances as of the "To" date, including regular and adjusting entries; owner's equity rolls forward over the period like the statement of owner's equity. Equipment, furniture, buildings, land, vehicles and accumulated depreciation are non-current assets; long-term loans, mortgages and bonds are non-current liabilities.

Statement of cash flows

Direct method, from every entry that touches a cash account (Asset accounts named Cash, Bank or Petty cash). Capital, drawings and loans/notes are financing; equipment, furniture, buildings, land and vehicles are investing; everything else is operating.
